# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Students and professors using Panthraa to manage classes, schedules, assignments, submissions, attendance, grades, announcements, and class communication from a mobile-first Android app.

## Product Purpose
Panthraa helps academic users see what needs attention now, move into the right class or task quickly, and keep coursework and class administration organized. Success means dashboard information is scannable, actions feel obvious, and repeated daily workflows stay calm and efficient.

## Brand Personality
Clear, helpful, and steady. The interface should feel friendly enough for students but structured enough for professor workflows.

## Anti-references
Avoid sketchy, unfinished UI language, over-heavy outlines, cramped text, unclear tap targets, and dashboard sections that visually fight each other.

## Design Principles
- Make the next useful action obvious without overwhelming the dashboard.
- Keep academic status information readable at a glance.
- Preserve mobile ergonomics with stable, comfortable tap targets.
- Use consistent component language across student and professor surfaces.
- Favor calm hierarchy over decorative styling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA contrast where practical, keep text legible on tinted surfaces, preserve reduced-motion friendliness, and avoid relying on color alone for status.
